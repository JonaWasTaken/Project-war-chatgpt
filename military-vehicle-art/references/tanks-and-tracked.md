# Tanks and tracked vehicles

## Identity and assemblies
Lock variant-specific hull/turret silhouette and equipment from references. Do not blend nearby variants. Refine hull and deck, turret/ring, visible gun/mantlet, running gear, skirts, tracks, then hatches/optics/equipment. Check barrel-to-turret proportion rather than merely making a long cylinder. Preserve identity over decorative equipment.

## Running gear
Place wheel centres and their widths against the approved side/front bounds. Establish end wheels and clearance to skirts. Design one track link at the useful detail level; distribute along a continuous path with controlled tangent orientation and pitch. Test closure, reversed links, intersections, skirt clearance, wheel fit, left/right alignment, and contact along the lower run. Recheck after wheel/hull edits. For approved detailed masters, the [track builder](track-builder.md) constructs supported static belts with actual linked mesh data, preserving master UV/material detail and reporting orientation/spacing diagnostics. It does not author a missing master, solve suspension, animate physical hinges, or prove exact collision/clearance. For close-up military simulation, follow the [close-up profile](close-up-production.md) and keep a high-detail source; evaluate simplification as a separate distant/runtime representation rather than discarding source detail.

## Surface and motion checks
Use side/front clay views to reveal hull depth, turret height, and width. Check turret sweep and gun elevation at representative poses; distinguish authorized game approximations from observed geometry. Check visible mounts and support. Isolate rubber, painted hubs, track surfaces, paint, and optics; avoid near-black running gear that hides construction. Do not add later-variant armour or sensors merely because a generated image contains them.

## Failure recovery
If it looks like a toy, compare primary ratios before adding bolts. If tracks look broken, inspect path/orientation/spacing before increasing resolution. If turret fits only in the forward pose, correct interfaces/pivots, not materials. If all detail disappears at RTS distance, adjust readable shape/material contrast rather than indiscriminate micro-detail.
