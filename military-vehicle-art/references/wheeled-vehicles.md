# Wheeled and support vehicles

Lock vehicle category, axle arrangement, body/cab proportions, and referenced equipment. Build chassis/body, wheels/arches, cab/glazing, support equipment, then secondary fittings. Check track width, wheelbase, axle alignment, tyre width, body overhang, and ground contact against supported ratios.

Use a consistent approved source tyre/hub for genuinely repeated components. Do not hide misaligned wheels under oversized mudguards. Check steering/wheel pivots and representative motion when required. Keep supports/equipment attached to coherent visible mounts; avoid a collection of unrelated boxes.

Inspect body shape under clay and grazing light. Differentiate rubber, paint, glass, grille/recesses, and equipment materials. Preserve existing animation or gameplay attachment names when improving a model. For support equipment, model its visible form and game poses without real military-system engineering.
