# Drones

Identify multirotor, fixed-wing, VTOL hybrid, or another specified design before choosing assemblies. Lock arm/wing arrangement, rotor count, body shape, landing supports, and camera/sensor housing from the brief/reference. Do not force every drone into a quadcopter template.

For multirotors, build a coherent central frame, repeated arms/motor housings where appropriate, propellers, mounts, and landing supports. Check rotor clearances, consistent propeller scale, visible arm connections, and sensor attachment. For fixed-wing, use fuselage sections, wings/tail, propulsion, and landing configuration from the aircraft workflow. A hybrid needs an explicit consistent arrangement, not concatenated generic parts.

Inspect top/front/side and intended-distance views. Correct fragile-looking floating motor pods, inconsistent repetition, zero-thickness wing forms, and opaque camera placeholders. Separate composites, plastic, exposed metals, and lenses where relevant. Test requested articulation/rotor motion visually; do not add operational flight controls or targeting engineering.

Missing image generation blocks concept generation, not a claim that a picture exists. Present a supplied-reference/manual concept route for permission rather than fabricating a generated image or silently sending private data to an external service.
