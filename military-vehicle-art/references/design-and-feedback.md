# Design authority, approval scope and feedback

## Route by intent

**Original/fictional:** the actually approved design brief, concept and authored invariants govern. Use reference objects for material/construction plausibility, not a compulsory photogrammetric target. An original desk lamp or fictional tank can be judged coherent without an exact real-world counterpart. Record authored dimensions as design decisions, not observed measurements. Generic words such as 'fictional' do not establish originality, licensing or freedom from legal risk.

**Reconstruction:** independently supported references govern the real subject/version/configuration. Keep unknown surfaces uncertain and independent checks separate from fit data. A failed registration cannot be escaped by silently relabelling the task fictional. A genuinely changed brief needs actual user authorization and appropriately separate truthful records.

**Existing refinement:** preserve supplied identity and accepted forms. Inspect the actual current asset, determine the failing system and make versioned targeted improvements. Do not force an approved existing model through another concept-generation stage.

**Stylization:** lock the intended proportions, exaggeration, surface/material language and viewing context. Stylized work still needs deliberate forms and coherent connections. Do not turn it into realism by default or use the style label to excuse accidental geometry.

Use the controller's actual documented intent/authorization records. Missing approval is missing; do not synthesize consent, a source baseline or hashes from a story about what probably happened.

## Diagnose a camera failure before interpreting it

A fitter can fail because of poor initialization, a mismatched fixed lens/crop, incompatible or inaccurate landmark annotations, limited image evidence, a local solver's limitations, inaccurate geometry, or a combination. Inspect the actual residuals, landmark placement, source/camera and producer report. Do not confidently select one cause without evidence.

Do not widen thresholds, refresh stale hashes or silently refit comparison cameras to manufacture acceptance. Show unfitted side-by-side views as qualitative comparisons, not registered overlays or numerical fidelity evidence. Matching sparse landmarks or a 2D outline does not prove complete volume/material detail.

For reconstruction, unresolved photographic checks limit accuracy claims; they are not proof that every part is wrong or a reason to restart everything. For original design, a photo fit to an unrelated real object is not an acceptance criterion. Locked orthographic/perspective design views still help compare versions and inspect approved design invariants.

## Match approval to stage and scope

- **Concept/design:** approve what will be made, configuration/style and deliberate departures; do not approve absent geometry.
- **Blockout:** approve coherent primary forms and major relationships, or specify conditional permission and editable uncertainties. Do not demand final textures, UVs, bolts or individual links here.
- **Benchmark/assemblies:** demand an actually finished representative region in whole-asset context. A grey blockout or a polished tiny detail is not sufficient.
- **Materials:** inspect actual UV/material boundaries, surface scale, neutral and different light responses. Fancy lighting is not material validation.
- **Delivery:** verify the actual requested files, dependencies, export/reimport and required motion/destination behavior. A pleasing render is not an engine test.

Conditional permission to progress is not an accuracy sign-off. Record accepted scope, remaining uncertainty, prohibited irreversible commitments and next review. Keep uncertain governing dimensions/interfaces editable; do not propagate expensive dependent details before their bounds are supported. If a mandatory coverage check genuinely fails, record failure: conditional language must not turn it into `pass`. A legitimate changed brief needs actual approval and a fresh review, not weakened historical evidence.

Do not hold a coherent blockout indefinitely for minor detail or require a new human approval for every local correction. Preserve the agreed concept/blockout/first-finished-asset gates and actual bounded delegation.

## Rejection becomes a bounded correction

1. Preserve the rejected version and the actual feedback reference. If 'no' is ambiguous, ask one short question about the concrete disliked feature; do not guess a new global style preference.
2. Classify the issue: primary shape, design intent, assembly fit, normals/bevel, UV, material, lighting, view or delivery behavior. Describe the visible observation separately from its suspected cause.
3. Select one high-impact hypothesis, expected visible outcome and affected neighbours. Keep camera/light/pose fixed unless they are the system being tested; label any intentional presentation change.
4. Make a new source version, generate actual after-images, inspect the changed region plus whole-object and regression views. Keep or revert based on evidence. A newer filename is not an improvement.
5. Record what happened in project-local notes. After two failed targeted attempts, change diagnosis/method or request missing evidence, not more random detail.

This is deliberately incomplete working-note shape, not an executable schema, approval record or success fixture. Store actual paths/fingerprints where useful; do not invent inspections. Keep taste-specific preferences and vehicle/object-specific corrections local unless the user explicitly generalizes them.

## Global updates are separate authorized maintenance

Project lessons are not model training and this skill does not autonomously improve its weights. Do not rewrite the global skill whenever a result is rejected. Propose a reusable rule only when evidence supports it; explain its scope and counterexamples. Obtain explicit authorization, back up outside discovery roots, add a regression where applicable, test actual affected workflows, review the change and keep rollback simple.

Never lower numerical thresholds, remove required views, replace references with the model's output, invent approval or promote inferred details to facts so rejected work can pass. Tests establish tested behavior, not the truth of an aesthetic judgment. Report what actually improved, what did not and what remains unverified.
