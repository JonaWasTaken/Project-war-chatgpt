# Aircraft construction and reference reconstruction

## Configuration and evidence

Lock exact aircraft variant, era/equipment, planform, engine arrangement and pose. Record gear, canopy, bays, stores and control-surface configuration; parked and flying photographs may show different relationships. Do not combine prototype geometry, later service equipment and unrelated markings because each reference is labelled with the same family name. Public photographs describe observable artwork, not classified internal construction or aerodynamic/stealth performance.

Use complementary front/side/top/rear/underside and three-quarter views plus connected detail crops. Drawings are not automatically dimensionally trustworthy; evaluate their source and agree them with photographs. Generated alternate views remain design hypotheses. Reserve checking views/landmarks not used to fit the main shape. Unknown lower surfaces, internal ducts and obscured joins remain explicitly uncertain.

For an original aircraft, the authorized concept and authored dimensions govern, not a spurious historical-fit score. Preserve requested deliberate stylization and approved identity.

## Choose construction by region

| Region | Construction approach | Inspect before panel lines |
|---|---|---|
| Fuselage/pod | Corresponding cross-sections, deliberate curve cage or suitable direct subdivision surface | Continuous profile, full front/top section, cross-section rotation, local pinching, silhouette shrinkage |
| Wing/fin/stabilator | Actual closed thickness profile across span stations; [lifting-surface builder](lifting-surfaces.md) where supported | Planform/sweep/taper, thickness distribution, twist, leading/trailing edges, root/tip caps |
| Wing/body or nacelle blend | Authored transition patch/edge flow or deliberately cleaned boolean union | Root curvature and highlight continuity in several views, no coincident shells or seam disguised by paint |
| Intake | Explicit lip, thickness and visible recess with reference-supported transition | Opening shape, section/depth, front/side relationships, inner visibility; not a black flat sticker |
| Canopy | Section-controlled transparent shell and separately controlled frame/seal | Profile, thickness, nose/body integration, reflections/transmission and visible cockpit |
| Exhaust | Reference-supported visible lip/nozzle structure, thickness and recess | Rear-view spacing and section, mounting, inside depth, not an unexplained dark polygon |
| Gear | Shared wheel/strut/door contract and real attachment origins | Ground contact, stance, axle orientation, door gaps and intermediate poses if motion is required |
| Pipes/hoses/handles | [Routed component builder](component-builders.md) or suitable deliberate curves | Endpoints, bend shape, circular section, local intersections and visible attachment |

The existing convex section loft cannot reconstruct an entire blended aircraft by itself. The new lifting-surface builder does not solve fuselage/wing blends, joints, control mechanisms or aerodynamics. Do not turn a successful helper call into a finished-aircraft claim.

## Ordered modelling passes

### 1. Whole-aircraft massing

Establish units, axes and a consistent frame, published dimensional baselines where supported, major proportions and complete wings/tails/engine/gear envelopes. Keep front/side/top relationships simultaneous. Do not extrude a generic fuselage tube then attach flat triangle wings.

Check saved clay and silhouette cameras from front, side, top, rear and useful three-quarter views. Do not add detail until major surface transitions and assembly placement are serviceable. Do not reject a legitimate blockout for absent fasteners, finished tyres or textures.

### 2. Deliberate thickness and curvature

Choose enough authored stations to control actual changes, not arbitrary density. Keep correspondence consistent along fuselage sections; do not rotate labels to force a lumpy bridge. For wing sections, actual normalized profile input and station controls must be supported by the evidence/design; a generic supplied profile is a modelling approximation, not the aircraft's real aerodynamic section.

Preserve intentional chines, facets, sharp break lines and thin trailing edges instead of smoothing everything. Avoid zero-thickness closed-volume claims. Inspect front/top/side curvature, local root thickness and camera-facing highlights after subdivision or bevels. More subdivision does not repair bad surface layout.

### 3. Representative difficult junction

Choose a junction such as canopy/intake/wing root that exposes the aircraft's hardest construction, not an isolated rivet. Finish it in whole-aircraft context: continuous broad surfaces, credible recesses, local thickness, attachment and material boundaries. Inspect neutral clay, grazing highlight and closest intended view. Propagate only the parts of the method that actually work.

### 4. Secondary assemblies and controlled details

Complete intakes, canopy/frame, engine/exhaust transitions, control-surface gaps, gear/doors and supported access panels. The [feature inventory](feature-inventory.md) connects specific visible observations to actual scene implementations. The [surface placement helper](surface-details.md) can position an inspected fastener/vent master on a named evaluated target, but cannot infer unseen hardware or guarantee full-footprint seating on curvature.

Use geometric seams when they affect silhouette or recess depth, mapped details when they do not. Panel lines must not be trenches. Avoid oversized rivets and random greebles; missing a real conspicuous feature is not repaired by adding ten invented ones. Recheck neighbours after a governing dimension changes. Preserve reference-supported asymmetry rather than mirroring all details blindly.

### 5. Neutral materials and actual scale

Separate painted/composite-looking exterior finishes, exposed metal, glazing, rubber and exhaust areas as supported by references. Material families are visible appearance choices, not proprietary material formulas. Avoid random per-panel colour patches, metallic body paint by default and opaque/glowing canopy glass. Test glazing against both light and dark background content; a black world can make valid glass unreadable.

Match coatings/markings to the selected configuration. Inspect real grain/noise/normal scale, density/stretch, seams and repeat patterns on the evaluated mesh. Perform locked-camera material-stress renders without hiding form in cinematic darkness. Regional photo-colour differences are not calibrated material measurements when illumination/white balance differs.

## Mechanical and delivery boundaries

If rigging/motion is requested, inspect representative and intermediate gear, door, stabilator and control-surface poses. Named pivot objects alone do not establish working animation. Static geometry need not acquire an invented full flight rig. Externally visible aircraft artwork is not a physically validated flight model.

Preserve high-detail editable source; author destination LOD/export derivatives separately when required. Re-import actual exports and inspect canopy/material compatibility, axes, scale, hierarchy and required animation in the requested destination. An arbitrary low polygon cap should not damage the close-up source.

Aircraft expertise is demonstrated by actual whole-aircraft results and matched reference evidence. Successful synthetic wing/component tests establish those construction operations, not a complete F-22, photorealism, historical exactness or universal plane-modelling mastery.
