# Artillery artwork

Distinguish towed, wheeled self-propelled, and tracked self-propelled configurations. Use the appropriate chassis/carriage, visible barrel/cradle form, shields if referenced, supports, wheels/tracks, and stowage. Model exterior visual forms and game animation only, not internal weapon mechanisms or real construction specifications.

Plan deployed/transport poses from references when requested. Keep support geometry visibly connected, consistent with the intended pose, and grounded. Check pivots and representative movement for clipping or detached assemblies; do not invent engineering limits. Reuse the tracked or wheeled approach where relevant without converting all configurations into the same tank-like silhouette.

Inspect profile proportions, carriage width, wheel/track alignment, visible mounts, ground contact, and readable assembly separation. Use controlled bevels and differentiate paint/rubber/exposed surfaces. Correct the main carriage silhouette before adding fittings. If the requested pose isn't supported by available references, label the inference and ask about consequential choices.
